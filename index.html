The approach

Code issues are easy to flag.
Harder to prioritize.

A warning is useful when a developer can understand its context, assess its impact, and decide what to do next. CodeSniff's direction is to make code-quality signals more actionable.

⌕

Surface code smells

Identify patterns that may indicate maintainability concerns, unnecessary complexity, or duplicated logic.

DETECTION · EXPLORING
◎

Explain why it matters

Translate technical findings into clear explanations so developers can understand potential risks and trade-offs.

A practical workflow

From noisy warnings to clearer decisions.

The product direction focuses on helping developers understand what a finding means, why it deserves attention, and how to approach a fix, without hiding the reasoning behind an opaque score.

Research-informed direction

Built around the questions behind code quality.

CodeSniff's direction connects practical developer tooling with empirical software engineering: how code smells vary in severity, how structural context influences their significance, and how teams can make better decisions about technical debt.
